About Us

At Crofiting Our aim is provide clean renewalble energy to the community and provide a cleaner, more affordable energy alternative. Our mission is to study how clean renewable solar energy can be used as a complimentary addition to agriculture, livestock, pollination, and everday life uses.

What Makes Us Different

Croftings aim is to study and learn ways to work with farmers and locals to ease their financial burdens while providing a high diversity in flora and fauna to create a cleaner enviornment for all. We are studing and working towards a system and solution where all of these things an directly benefit each other, because we believe if you can help even just one person you help everyone.
